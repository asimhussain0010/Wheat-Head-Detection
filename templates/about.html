About the project

Wheat Head Detection, explained

This project applies a YOLOv5 object detector — enhanced with separable convolutions — to identify and count wheat heads in field photography, supporting yield estimation and crop research workflows.

Technology stack

PyTorch, YOLOv5, OpenCV, and Flask power the model training pipeline and this web interface.

Dataset

Trained on thousands of annotated field images captured under varied lighting, density, and occlusion conditions.

Goal

Give researchers and farmers a fast, accessible way to estimate wheat head counts from a single photo.

Pipeline overview

Field images are annotated with bounding boxes and fed into a YOLOv5 backbone modified with depthwise-separable convolutions to reduce parameter count. Training uses standard augmentation (mosaic, flip, HSV jitter) with an SGD optimizer.

At inference, an uploaded image is resized, passed through the network, and non-max suppression filters overlapping boxes. The rendered result overlays a bounding box on every detected wheat head.
